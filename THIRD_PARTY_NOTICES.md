# Third-party notices

No third-party runtime artifact, image, font, video or downloadable release is
currently approved in this public channel.

Before third-party material is published, its manifest entry must identify it
as either:

- `LICENSED_THIRD_PARTY` with `LICENSED_FOR_PUBLICATION`; or
- `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` with `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` rights status.

The release owner must retain the corresponding license or provenance evidence
outside the public payload. This file must be updated for every affected
release.
